# Invoicing Networx

**Why:** MSA §4 — Affiliate is paid only for Compliant Traffic accepted by Networx, and **Affiliate
must submit an accurate invoice monthly by the 15th day following the month of delivery**. Networx
pays undisputed amounts within 30 days of receiving the invoice. Networx may reject or charge back
non-compliant leads up to 60 days after delivery (up to two years for fraud, consent defects, privacy
or regulatory matters). **Disputes must be raised with supporting records within 30 days or are waived.**
Payment method on file: PayPal, ken.hoven@gmail.com.

## Monthly routine (do between the 1st and the 10th)
1. In the Networx affiliate dashboard, pull the prior month's report: accepted leads, rejected leads,
   payout per lead, total. Export it and save as `invoices/YYYY-MM-networx-report.csv` (or PDF).
2. Reconcile: total accepted leads × payout = invoice amount. If the dashboard shows rejections you
   disagree with, raise them with Josh **now** — the 30-day dispute window runs from the report.
3. Issue the invoice (template below) as PDF, numbered `NX-YYYY-MM`, dated, and email it to
   AccountsPayable@networx.com with josh@networx.com in copy. Subject:
   `Invoice NX-YYYY-MM — Kenneth Hoven / FixItReal — [Month YYYY] leads`.
4. Save the PDF and the sent email in `invoices/`. Record the invoice in `invoices/ledger.csv`.
5. Diary +30 days: confirm the PayPal payment arrived; if not, follow up with AccountsPayable.
6. Watch for chargebacks in the following 60 days; if any, dispute within 30 days with records or
   accept and note them in the ledger.

Networx is a US business paying a US individual; expect a Form 1099-NEC for the year if payments
reach the IRS threshold, and they may request a W-9 even for PayPal payouts. Keep the ledger so the
income is reportable regardless.
