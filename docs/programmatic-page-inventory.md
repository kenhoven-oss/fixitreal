## How to read this

- **Unique %**: share of a page's 8-word phrases that are NOT common to 80%+ of pages in its template family. Numbers and place names count as unique here.
- **Unique after normalisation %**: the same measure after every dollar figure, number and place name is replaced by a token. This is the honest number: it is what is left once "same template, different state and prices" is discounted. Google's scaled-content guidance is about this number, not the first one.
- **State/city-specific info**: which page elements carry information that could not be derived from the national guide plus a multiplier.
- **Sources**: every page cites its parent national guide and the tier model in its methodology block; state pages additionally cite the licensing body. No page carries a local quote sample.
- Nothing in this report changes any URL. See the recommendation at the end.
